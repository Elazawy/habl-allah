# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Arabic-speaking Muslim families, primarily Egyptian, browsing on mid-range Android phones. They arrive with a simple intent: find the right learning path for themselves or their children. They are not researchers — they are parents, students, and seekers who need a clear, confident answer in seconds. Many use WhatsApp daily and are comfortable with modern mobile UIs, but low-end devices and variable connections are common.

## Product Purpose

Habl Allah's landing page is a routing surface, not an information page. Its job is to get the right visitor to the right platform in one confident decision:

- **حبل الله** — the adult Quran learning platform (tajweed, recitation, certified courses)
- **أسس حبل الله** — the children's Islamic foundation platform (fiqh, aqeedah, seerah, interactive games)

Success is a visitor who arrives, instantly understands the split, and clicks through to the correct platform. No lingering, no confusion, no form to fill.

## Brand Personality

Accessible, warm, modern. Quran education is for everyone — not a privilege reserved for the already-devout or the formally educated. The brand should feel like a knowledgeable, welcoming teacher, not an institution issuing certificates. Gravity without heaviness. Confidence without intimidation.

## Anti-references

- Traditional Islamic educational sites: heavy dark mosque-green, gold ornamental borders, clipart mosque imagery, dense Arabic text in Times New Roman — this brand rejects that register entirely.
- Generic SaaS landing pages with "hero + 3 feature cards + testimonials + CTA" formula — the brand is not a startup, it is an academy with a specific purpose.
- Childish double-register designs where the kids section degrades the overall brand — both portals must feel premium; the children's portal is joyful, not cheap.
- Metric theater: rows of big numbers (500+ students, 30+ teachers, 10+ years) used to manufacture trust instead of demonstrating it.

## Design Principles

1. **One decision, made clearly.** The page exists to split traffic. The two portals are the hero; everything else is context that earns the click.
2. **Warmth without weight.** Islamic values are present in the tone and copy, not in ornamental tile patterns and heavy dark palettes. Modern materials, genuine warmth.
3. **Mobile-first Egyptian context.** Every layout decision should be tested at 360–390px first. Thumb-zone CTAs, fast load, no reliance on hover.
4. **Both portals are premium.** Adults and children get equal visual quality. The kids portal is playful; it is not lesser.
5. **Earned trust.** Any credential shown (years, students, teachers) must feel plausible and specific, not a stat counter row. If it cannot be made to feel real, remove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. RTL layout throughout — no mixed-direction assumptions. Arabic numerals in UI stats (٠١٢٣...) where culturally appropriate. Font size floor of 16px for body text. Reduced-motion support already present; maintain it. Keyboard navigation must work (tab order follows visual reading order in RTL).
